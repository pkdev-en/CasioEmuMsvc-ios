# 📲 Guide to installing CasioEmuMsvc on iOS using SideStore

> This guide is for **iPhone/iPad** users who want to install a Casio emulator for iOS.

---

## 📋 Requirements

- iPhone/iPad running **iOS 14 or later**

- **Apple ID** (a free Apple account is sufficient)
- **SideStore** installed and set up on the device
- **CasioEmuMsvc.ipa** file (download from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/pkdev-en/CasioEmuMsvc-ios/releases))

---

## 🔧 Step 1 — Install SideStore (if not already installed)

> If you already have SideStore, skip this step and go straight to Step 2.

Go to [SlideStore.io](https://sidestore.io) and follow the instructions.

---

## 📥 Step 2 — Download the IPA file

1. Access the **Releases** page of the repo:

👉 [github.com/pkdev-en/CasioEmuMsvc-ios/releases](https://github.com/pkdev-en/CasioEmuMsvc-ios/releases)

2. Download the latest version of the **`CasioEmuMsvc`** file.

3. Save the file to **Files → On this iPhone** (or iCloud Drive).

---

## 📲 Step 3 — Installing IPA using SideStore

1. Open the **SideStore** app on your iPhone.

3. Switch to the **My Apps** tab (app icon in the bottom bar).

4. Tap the **＋** sign in the upper right corner.

5. Select the file you just downloaded → tap **Open**.

6. Log in with your Apple ID if prompted, then tap **Install**.

7. Wait a few seconds — the app will appear on your home screen.

---

## ✅ Step 4 — First Launch

1. Open **CasioEmuMsvc** from the home screen.

2. Click the **Import** button.

3. Select the Casio emu you want to import (with the .package extension).

4. Enter your password.

5. After entering your password, click **all** and you will see your emu.

6. Click on your chosen emu and click launch.

7. Enjoy! 🥳

---
# NOTE:

## 🔄 Renew your app (avoid expiring after 7 days)

Free Apple ID only signs apps valid for **7 days**. To renew:

- Open **SideStore**, connect to your **LocalDevVPN** VPN, and go to **My Apps**.

- Tap **Refresh All** or tap the app name and select **Refresh**.

- Turn on **Background Refresh** in SideStore Settings for automatic renewal.

> **Tip:** Use **LiveContainer** if you want to install more apps than the 3-app limit of your free Apple ID :)

---

## ❗ Common Errors

| Error | Solution |

|-----|-----------|

| App deleted after 7 days | Renew via SideStore before expiration |

| Cannot connect to SideStore VPN | Check **pairing file** in SideStore Settings |

| Error "App could not be installed" | Ensure you trust the certificate in iOS Settings |
